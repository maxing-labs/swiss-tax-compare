    About Swiss Tax Compare

    Data source

    Every figure comes live from the tax calculator of the Swiss Federal Tax Administration (FTA / ESTV), the same engine behind swisstaxcalculator.estv.admin.ch. Tariffs and multipliers for 2010–2026 are covered for every municipality.

    Independent · No ads

    No advertising, no affiliation with the FTA or any adviser, no tracking. Saved scenarios stay in your own browser and are never uploaded.

    How it is calculated

    With gross income the FTA calculator deducts social contributions (AHV/IV/EO, ALV, NBU, BVG) and the standard professional and insurance deductions, then applies the federal, cantonal, municipal and church tariffs. With taxable income nothing is deducted. The effective rate is total tax divided by the income you entered; the marginal rate is the tax on the next franc.

    For guidance only

    These are estimates: individual deductions, special tariffs and rounding differ. Your cantonal tax office's assessment is authoritative. Nothing here is tax advice.
